Color adds brightness and depth to your natural shade and lets you try something new.

    $89+

  • All-Over Color

    $108+

    Color applied to all your hair, from roots to ends, for an even shade and a natural or dramatic change.

  • Partial Highlights

    $125+

    Highlights on selected sections, usually on top and at the sides, for a sun-kissed look with more depth.

  • Full Highlights

    $160+

    Highlights throughout all your hair, from roots to ends, for a lighter look with more dimension.

  • Multi-Dimensional Highlights

    $168+

  • Babylights

    $200+

  • Partial Babylights

  • Vivid

  • Color Correction

    Fixes earlier color mistakes, such as uneven or unwanted tones. Consultation required to choose the best approach.

  • Glazing Toner

    $88+

  • Money Pieces

    $68+
