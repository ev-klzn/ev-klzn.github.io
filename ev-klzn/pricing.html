FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a website project usually take?
It depends on the complexity and scope of the project and on your requirements. There’s no single answer, so we discuss everything with you openly and agree on a realistic timeline that suits your needs. Our goal is to deliver quality work efficiently, with care and attention to detail at every step.
What does a fixed-price website mean?
It means the cost of the website is set and agreed before the project starts, however much time or resources it takes. You know the total cost upfront, with no surprises or extra fees during development.
Can you help with maintenance and updates after the project is finished?
Absolutely! We offer website maintenance and updates to keep your website secure, up to date and running well after launch. We can update content, add new features, work on SEO or fix any technical issues. A website needs regular attention, and we’re here to keep it working for you long after launch.
What is basic SEO optimisation?
Basic SEO optimisation covers semantic web development, writing meta tags, registering your website in Google Search Console and Google Analytics, and generating a sitemap. Further SEO support is available on request: we research the best keywords and strategies to improve your search rankings.
What is your website design and development process?
We start with a discovery call to understand your goals and requirements. After research and planning, we move on to design and development, creating wireframes, prototypes and design concepts that improve user experience and conversions. Before launch, we test everything carefully so your website is ready to go.
