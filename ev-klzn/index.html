The Process

Your Website in 5 Steps

Our clear process gives you a website built around your business needs

  1. 2 hours
    DO WE MATCH?

    Virtual Meeting

    Before we start, we find out how we can help you best. What do you need from your new website, and why do you need it now? Once we know your goals and challenges, we can offer solutions that fit them.

    Get to know each other
    Find the best ways we can support you
    Understand what you want your website to achieve
  2. 1 week
    WE NEED A PLAN

    Concept & Strategy

    Together we create a strategy that connects your goals with the needs of your target audience. Then we build the first wireframes and an interactive prototype, so you can see how the design and interface will work.

    UX Design
    Wireframes
    Interactive Prototype
  3. 1–2 weeks
    Some Magic

    Web Design

    The magic begins. Based on the agreed concept, we create a high-quality screen design made for your brand. It sets you apart from competitors, speaks to your target audience and gives users a great experience.

    Premium web design made for your brand
    Interactive prototype of the design
  4. ≈ 1–2 weeks
    More Magic

    Web Development

    Now we bring your new design to life. You get a custom website built with a modular system and connected to a CMS. Dynamic animations add character and set you apart from competitors.

    Modular web development system
    CMS integration
  5. 2 hours
    Ready to go

    Website Onboarding

    First, we publish your website so your audience can find it. After the launch, we hold a one-on-one workshop and show you how to update it yourself: how to use the content management system (CMS), change text and images, and keep your website up to date.

    Personal workshop
    Edit text and images directly on your website
