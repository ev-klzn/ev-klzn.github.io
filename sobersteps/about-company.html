About Us

Our Mission

Our mission is to use modern behavioral science to help people take control of their habits and change their lives. By combining cognitive behavioral therapy with mobile apps, we give people the tools and support to overcome problems like alcohol dependence. We want science-based, effective help to be available to everyone and to bring lasting positive change.

Psychology

Our products are built on evidence-based methods and scientifically proven principles, including cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), to help people build healthy habits for life.

Technology

We keep improving our products so our users always have the most effective tools for their health.

Healthier Life

We believe that people who use our app improve their quality of life and live fuller, richer lives. We want to help everyone reach their full potential with modern, effective solutions.

Our Team

We are a team of young professionals at AfterMind Lab Ltd, based in Cyprus. Our work is based on research published in scientific journals and on advice from psychotherapists. We combine up-to-date knowledge with practical experience, and scientific accuracy is at the heart of everything we do.
