Photos of the Bombik art space

We held our events in a proper bomb shelter in the Pokrovskyi district of Kryvyi Rih, so people could get a break even during an air raid siren.

These photos show what volunteers and artists made of the place. Paintings by Kryvyi Rih artist Serhii Areshchenko went up on the walls, along with a photo installation by Anatolii Novak and a mural by Andrii Kalinkin: a huge eye in the colours of the Ukrainian flag. There was a stage, lighting, chairs and a few comfy sofas, so it stopped feeling like an ordinary shelter.
