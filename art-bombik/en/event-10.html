“Anti-Halloween” evening

Our brilliant mimes, Yuliia and Maryna, took the Bombik stage with their “Anti-Halloween” programme. What stayed with people from that night was the striking make-up, the comic characters and the odd feeling of watching it all underground.

Format
mime programme and live music
On stage
mimes Yuliia and Maryna, musicians
Venue
bomb shelter in the Pokrovskyi district of Kryvyi Rih
Entry
free, donations collected

What the photos show

The mimes performed in dark costumes, and one of them came out with devil horns and red gloves. The hall was decorated with red and orange balloons and brooms, and plenty of guests turned up in hats and costumes.

There was live music as well, from guitarists and a drummer. In the group selfie you can see people holding up the donations they’d collected.
