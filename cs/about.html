Creations Studio:
Creating Unique, Sustainable Spaces Since 2008

Our mission is to design unique, comfortable spaces for living and working that are both beautiful and practical, with a personal approach to every client.

We use eco-friendly materials and modern technologies to improve our clients’ quality of life and create sustainable spaces.

We Specialize In

  • Architecture

    Good architectural design matters for any construction or renovation, whether it’s a home or a commercial space. When we design a house, we pay attention to:

    • Function and looks: layouts that meet the residents’ needs and an attractive design in line with modern architecture.
    • Sustainability and compliance: energy-efficient projects with eco-friendly materials and technologies that follow local building codes and regulations.
    • Community and safety: homes that fit naturally into their surroundings and are accessible and safe for everyone who lives there.
    Book a consultation

  • Interior Design

    Interior design is the stage that makes your life more comfortable, brighter and happier.

    When we design the interior of an apartment, house, cottage or office, we focus on:

    • Function and flow: layouts that use the space well and make it easy to move around, so every area does its job.
    • Style and personalization: designs with visual harmony that reflect the tastes of the people who live there.
    • Comfort and sustainability: good lighting and ergonomics for well-being, and eco-friendly materials for a healthier home.
    Book a consultation

  • Furniture & Decor

    Furniture and decor shape the whole atmosphere of a space. At this stage, we work closely with the client to choose furniture and decor that match the chosen style and vision. We focus on:

    • Quality and durability: high-quality materials and finishes that look good and last for years.
    • Color and theme: every element fits the project’s color palette and overall style.
    • Functional design: furniture that looks good and meets the practical needs of the space and the people who use it.
    Book a consultation
