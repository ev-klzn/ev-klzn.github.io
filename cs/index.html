Our Story

Creations Studio was founded in 2008 by a group of like-minded people who wanted to create unique, comfortable spaces for living and working. The start wasn’t easy, but thanks to a talented team of designers and architects, within a few years the studio became popular with clients who valued a personal approach and professionalism.

Since then, we have completed many successful projects, from small apartments to large commercial spaces. In every project we combine beauty and function and take the client’s needs and wishes into account. Eco-friendly materials and technologies that improve our clients’ quality of life are an important part of our approach.

Design Services

Architecture

Good architectural design matters for any construction or renovation, whether it’s a home or a commercial space. When we design a house, we pay attention to:

  • Function and looks: layouts that meet the residents’ needs and an attractive design in line with modern architecture.
  • Sustainability and compliance: energy-efficient projects with eco-friendly materials and technologies that follow local building codes and regulations.

Interior Design

Interior design is the stage that makes your life more comfortable, brighter and happier.

When we design the interior of an apartment, house, cottage or office, we focus on:

  • Function and flow: layouts that use the space well and make it easy to move around, so every area does its job.
  • Comfort and sustainability: good lighting and ergonomics for well-being, and eco-friendly materials for a healthier home.

Furniture & Decor

Furniture and decor shape the whole atmosphere of a space. At this stage, we work closely with the client to choose furniture and decor that match the chosen style and vision. We focus on:

  • Quality and durability: high-quality materials and finishes that look good and last for years.
  • Functional design: furniture that looks good and meets the practical needs of the space and the people who use it.

Cozy Scandinavian Kitchen Retreat

The open plan is built around a central island used both for cooking and for casual meals.

Cozy Scandinavian Kitchen Retreat

The design captures the essence of Scandinavian style: simplicity, practicality and warmth. The kitchen uses a light palette of soft whites and pale grays that feels airy, with subtle pastel accents for a touch of color. The open plan is built around a central island used both for cooking and for casual meals. Soft patterned fabrics make the kitchen feel welcoming, a perfect mix of form and function. It’s a kitchen made for family get-togethers and cooking adventures, in the true Scandinavian spirit of simplicity and comfort.
