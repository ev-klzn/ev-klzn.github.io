Works

Recent projects

Here are some of my recent video projects, including work with Proximus Media House, Eleven, PRG and Novid/AllRent.

25 Years Orange

Live visual mixing
Screen content management
Together with Novid and Balthazar Events, I helped celebrate the 25th anniversary of Belgian telecom company Orange. The event took place in a large tent at De Schorre in Boom, with a big LED wall and delay screens. We put the photos from the photographers on site straight into the show, and I mixed all the visuals on the screens.

Proximus Media House

Live visual mixing
Multi-session coordination
At Proximus Media House, I handle the recording, archiving, streaming and management of all media content made by PMH and its clients. Most of my work is sport: Belgian and European football, volleyball, hockey, cycling and basketball. I work with tools like EVS IP-Director and different streaming platforms to make sure everything runs smoothly.

BAM

LED screen content curation
Speaker collaboration
Technical production
Together with AllRent, I helped run the well-known BAM Marketing Congress. We used LED walls and projector blends to create a strong visual setup. I prepared and managed all the content for the LED screens, including one setup with more than 8 million pixels, and worked closely with the keynote speakers so their content looked exactly the way they wanted.

Cage Warriors

Technical event support
Screen content management
In 2017 I was the technical producer for Eleven at the Cage Warriors MMA event in the Lotto Arena in Antwerp. From the OB van, I managed the graphics, video and timings so the show ran without a hitch.

MDL Beast

Visual production
Screen content management
At MDL Beast, the pioneering dance festival in the Saudi Arabian desert, my PRG team and I built the Techno stage. We fitted 18 LED screens into shipping containers and set the stage up to run on Resolume, all in tough desert conditions. The result was one synchronised LED display, and being part of this historic event was unforgettable.

Antwerp Symphony Orchestra

Live visual mixing
Screen content management
Speaker collaboration
Event technology innovation

During the Covid-19 pandemic, I helped VideoCrew produce livestreamed concerts of the Antwerp Symphony Orchestra. As chief technician, I managed all audio and video signals to keep the quality high. It felt good to help bring music to people at such a difficult time.

Salesforce Live

Live visual mixing
Screen content management

Every year I work on Salesforce Live, the main event of Salesforce Belgium, with production support from Blue Moon. It’s an important meeting place for the Salesforce community.

At the event, I run the video mixing for several setups, including a 3-projector blend and multiple LED walls. After the main keynotes, guests join three workshops at the same time with silent disco headphones, and I run all the sessions in parallel.

Working on this event every year is a great experience, and I look forward to the next edition.

Master Control Room Operator at VRT

Live visual mixing
Screen content management

As an MCR operator at VRT, I help keep broadcasting smooth and reliable for viewers across Belgium. I switch, monitor and stream all incoming feeds that arrive by satellite, microwave links and the internet. I work with the sports, news and TV production teams, so every department gets the files and feeds it needs quickly and easily.

I’m also involved in VRT’s move to a new building with the latest technology. I help introduce new solutions that will make the broadcaster’s work more efficient and ready for the future.
