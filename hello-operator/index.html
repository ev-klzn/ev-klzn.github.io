Who I am

Welcome to Hello, Operator!

Need a reliable video operator? Welcome to Hello, Operator, your partner for smooth visual productions. I’m a video operator based in Belgium, and I work on broadcasts and live events.

I have years of experience with presentation switchers such as Barco E2, Pixelhue, and Vmix, so I can run the video side of live productions, corporate events and congresses without a hitch. Whether it’s a high-stakes broadcast or a polished business presentation, I adapt to the way your team works and help bring your ideas to life.

I also offer equipment rental for your production, so you get reliable gear and someone who knows how to use it. Whether you need an experienced video technician or the latest equipment, I’m here to help make your event a success.

At Hello, Operator, every frame and every detail matters. Let’s make your next production a success together.
