Story of Furry Oasis

Welcome to Furry Oasis, where grooming is more than a service: it’s a treat for your pet! Since 2016 we’ve been providing high-quality grooming with a gentle, loving touch. What started as a small dream of two animal lovers has become a trusted name in pet care in our community.

We believe every pet deserves to look and feel its best. Our skilled groomers specialize in stress-free grooming and choose products and techniques for each pet’s needs. From refreshing baths and stylish trims to spa treatments and mobile services, we make every visit comfortable, safe and pleasant.

We’re more than a grooming salon. We’re a place where tails wag, coats shine and pets leave happy and confident. Join the Furry Oasis family and let us give your pet the care it deserves!

Our approach

Furry Oasis

We’ve created a cozy, luxurious space where your pet gets excellent care and a bit of pampering. We combine quality grooming with a personal approach, so every pet looks and feels its best. We use only premium products, modern equipment and gentle techniques to keep the coat healthy, shiny and soft. It’s not only about looks, but about your pet’s well-being.

Every pet is unique, so we take time to understand its character and needs. Our experienced groomers work patiently and carefully to keep the visit calm and stress-free. For us, grooming is a moment of love and care that leaves your pet happy, relaxed and looking fabulous.

Hygiene and sterilization

Your pet’s health is our top priority, so we pay special attention to cleanliness and safety. Disinfection and sterilization are part of our daily work and keep every four-legged guest comfortable and protected.

We take care of:
  • Sanitizing all surfaces after each visit;
  • Sterilizing tools after each client;
  • Using disposable materials for hygiene procedures;
  • Regular antibacterial and antiparasitic treatment of the whole salon.

When you leave your pet with us, you can be sure it’s in a clean, safe and comfortable place!
