Our services

From idea to a working website

  • web design UI/UX

    Clear, easy-to-use design
    • 01

      Concept

      We start by learning about your business and your customers. Based on that, we come up with the concept and draw wireframes: simple page layouts that show what goes where before the design work begins.

      • Research

        We look at your audience, your competitors and your market to understand what people need.

      • Site structure

        We plan the pages, the menu and how the pages link to each other.

      • UX strategies

        We work out how people will move through the site and what they should be able to do on it.

      • Wireframes

        Simple sketches of each page that show where the content goes.

    • 02

      UI/UX Design

      We design an interface that’s easy to use and looks the way your brand should look. Every element is there for a reason, from colours to button sizes.

      • UX analysis

        If you already have a site or an app, we check what works for users and what gets in their way.

      • UI design

        Buttons, fonts, colours, icons and the rest of the visual details.

      • Branding

        Colour palette, fonts, logo and the overall style of your brand.

      • infographics

        Charts and visuals that make numbers and data easier to understand.

      • adaptation

        We adjust layouts and content so they work well on different screens and platforms.

      • documentation

        Clear notes for developers, so the finished site matches the design exactly.

    • 03

      Prototype

      Before launch, you can click through an interactive prototype and see how the site will work. It’s much easier to change things at this stage than after the code is written.

      • Prototyping

        Clickable prototypes to test the idea and get feedback early.

      • Functionality

        We agree on which features the site needs and how they should work.

      • Testing

        We try the site out in real use to make sure it does what you and your visitors expect.

  • web development

    Fast, reliable websites
    • 01

      Modular Strategy

      We build websites from flexible, reusable blocks. This makes development faster, and later you can update or extend the site without rebuilding it from scratch.

      • Flexibility

        Each block can be adjusted to your needs, so the site can change as your business changes.

      • Scalability

        Adding new pages, features or content is easy and doesn’t break what’s already there.

      • Faster Development

        We reuse blocks that are already built and tested, so we finish sooner without cutting corners.

      • Cost Efficiency

        Less code written from scratch means a lower price for you.

      • Ease of Maintenance

        To change something, we edit one block instead of digging through the whole site.

    • 02

      Clean code

      We write code using the BEM methodology. It keeps the code simple and easy to read, which means a faster site, fewer bugs and easier changes later.

      • Readability

        Any developer can open the code and quickly understand how it’s built.

      • Maintainability

        Clean code is easy to fix and change without breaking something else.

      • Efficiency

        No unnecessary code means pages load faster.

      • Scalability

        Well-organised code makes it easy to add features or handle more visitors without a major rebuild.

      • Fewer Bugs

        Simple code leaves fewer places for errors to hide, so the site keeps working reliably.

    • 03

      CMS Integration

      We connect your site to a CMS, a simple admin panel where you can update texts and images yourself. No coding needed, and no waiting for a developer.

      • User-Friendly

        Change texts, photos and other content yourself, without any coding skills.

      • Scalability

        Add new pages and sections as your business grows.

      • Customizable

        We set up the CMS around your site and your tasks, with nothing you don’t need.

      • SEO-Friendly

        Most CMS platforms have built-in SEO tools that help with search rankings.

      • Content Control

        You’re in full control of your content and can publish changes right away.

  • seo optimization

    More visitors from Google
    • 01

      Comprehensive SEO Audits

      We go through your site page by page and find what keeps it from ranking higher. Then we make a plan of what to fix first.

      • Technical Analysis

        Slow pages, broken links, crawl errors and other technical problems that hurt SEO.

      • Content Review

        We check whether your content is useful, relevant and written the way search engines expect today.

      • Keyword Analysis

        Which searches you show up for now, and which ones are worth targeting.

      • Competitor Benchmarking

        What your competitors do in search, and where you can get ahead of them.

      • Backlink Evaluation

        Which sites link to you, which links help and which ones hurt your site’s reputation.

    • 02

      On-Page Optimization

      We improve each page on its own: the text, headings, meta tags and internal links. The page becomes clearer for search engines and more useful for visitors.

      • Meta Tag Optimization

        Page titles and descriptions that tell Google what the page is about and make people want to click in search results.

      • Content Optimization

        Clear structure, relevant text and the right keywords in the right places.

      • URL Structure

        Short, readable page addresses that make sense to people and search engines.

      • Internal Linking

        Links between your pages that help visitors find related content and help Google understand your site.

      • Image Optimization

        Proper file names and alt text, so images show up in search and are accessible to everyone.

    • 03

      Link Building and Authority

      Links from other good websites tell Google that your site can be trusted. We help you get those links, and over time your site gets more visitors from search.

      • Backlink Acquisition

        Links from reputable websites that strengthen your site’s authority.

      • Diverse Link Profile

        Links from different kinds of sources: blogs, news sites and industry directories.

      • Guest Blogging Opportunities

        We find relevant blogs where you can publish an article and get a link back to your site.

      • Content Marketing

        Useful content that other sites want to link to on their own.

      • Monitoring and Reporting

        We keep an eye on your links and send clear reports, so you can see what’s working and what to change.
